# DESIGO® Style Recommendation

Written 2026-10-01, after all 36 style plans were finished. For DESIGO® founders and the design team.

## The short answer
Don't build 36 websites. Build **one DESIGO® system**: two backbone styles, a few chapter accents, and the rest
used for one-off campaign pages. Every style document still contains a full 20-phase plan, so any of them
can be built later as a campaign or an alternative direction.

## 1. Backbone (the whole site)
| Role | Style | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Structure & product | **15 Luxury Typography** (5/5) | Huge "26 · 14 · 3 · E" numerals with the real bottle in front. Expensive-looking and specific to DESIGO®. |
| Story & voice | **16 Editorial Design** (5/5) | A magazine about one bottle of milk. It carries origin, heritage and history honestly. |
| Base rules | **01 Minimalism** (5/5) | Space, restraint and one object per screen. This is what makes the other styles look premium. |

This is the direction the current demo already uses at `http://localhost:3210`.

# 17 — Y2K Aesthetic · DESIGO® build plan

**Fit score: 2 / 5 for the whole site** · **Best used for:** a time-boxed youth campaign page, for example
`/glass-is-back` about the returnable-glass loop, plus social and Instagram assets. Inside the main site, at most a
restrained "translucent" treatment of the four-milk selector.

---

## 1. Style essence

Y2K is the optimism of 1998–2004 design: translucent coloured plastic (the iMac G3 and the Game Boy Color), liquid
chrome, iridescent and holographic foil, bubble and blob shapes, glossy gel buttons, lens flares, wide extended
typefaces and "millennium" futurism. Today it returns via Gen-Z fashion and music branding as a knowing, joyful
maximalism.

Three reference points:
1. **Apple iMac G3 and the "Bondi Blue" era**: translucent, candy-coloured objects you could see inside.
   This is the direct link to a *glass bottle you can see the milk through*.
2. **Designers Republic and early-2000s music packaging** (Björk *Homogenic*-era, Bollywood film-title chrome
   graphics of the early 2000s).
3. **Contemporary revival:** Gen-Z fashion e-commerce, and Indian indie streetwear labels using chrome type.

## 2. Why it fits DESIGO® and where it fights

**Fits (narrowly)**
- **Translucency.** DESIGO® uses glass, and Y2K loved see-through objects. The caps (green, red, amber, ivory) read
  like a candy-coloured product family, the iMac's "flavours".
- **Return-the-glass behaviour** needs a young, habitual audience. A playful campaign can make returning glass feel cool.
- Shareable social assets (chrome "26", iridescent "14").

**Fights (broadly)**
- Y2K is synthetic: chrome, plastic, artificial gloss. DESIGO®'s promise is *natural, indigenous, traced*.
- It dates fast. A heritage brand needs longevity, while Y2K is a trend cycle.
- Glossy gradients plus food can look like a soft-drink brand, which cheapens milk and wrongly positions the product as a sugary drink.

**Verdict: 2/5.** Do not build the core site in Y2K. Build one **campaign micro-site** and a social kit, where the
retro-futurist optimism is aimed squarely at the returnable glass loop. The plan below is written for the full site
as requested, with explicit "campaign scope" markers.

## 3. Art direction

### Palette: "Milk Chrome"
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--y2k-milk` | `#F7F4EC` | Base (keeps the brand warm, not the cold #EEF Y2K white) |
| `--y2k-frost` | `#E6EEF0` | Frosted panels (cool tint for contrast) |
| `--chrome-hi` | `#FFFFFF` | Chrome highlight stop |
| `--chrome-mid` | `#B9C2C4` | Chrome mid stop |
| `--chrome-lo` | `#4E585A` | Chrome shadow stop |
| `--iri-1` | `#D9E8DF` | Iridescent stop (MASTER light) |
| `--iri-2` | `#F3D9D6` | Iridescent stop (ROOT light) |
| `--iri-3` | `#F8E4C2` | Iridescent stop (BASE light) |
| `--forest` | `#0B3B32` | Text, outlines, grounding |
| `--green` | `#1E7A68` | Primary actions |
| `--charcoal` | `#171918` | Dark sections |

Variant "flavours" (translucent gels, 72% opacity over frost): MASTER `#1F5C45`, ROOT `#B3202A`, BASE `#E89A1C`,
ESSENTIAL `#CDB89A`. The iridescent gradient is *built only from the four variant light tones*, so even the holographic
effect is on-brand: `linear-gradient(115deg, #D9E8DF, #F3D9D6 35%, #F8E4C2 65%, #F4EDE2)`.

### Typography
- **Display:** *Unbounded* (OFL, variable, wide), weights 300–800, for chrome numerals and campaign headlines.
- **Secondary display:** *Syne* (OFL) 700 for kickers, which gives an art-school Y2K edge.
- **Body:** *Inter Tight* 16/26. Body stays sober.
- **Data:** *Space Mono* (OFL) for bottle IDs, a period-appropriate monospace.
- **Brand sentences:** *Fraunces* italic remains for any heritage line, a deliberate warm counterpoint.
- **Devanagari:** *Baloo 2* (Ek Type, OFL), rounded and friendly, which works with blob shapes.

### Texture, imagery, iconography
- **Chrome** is achieved with SVG gradients and `feSpecularLighting` on type, never with bitmap chrome images.
- **Frosted panels:** `backdrop-filter: blur(18px) saturate(1.2)` with a 1 px white inner stroke and 24 px radius.
  Y2K is the one style where rounded corners are allowed, via a style-scoped token override `--r-y2k: 24px`.
- **Blobs:** 3–5 freeform SVG shapes per scene, morphing slowly (shape interpolation), coloured from the variant gels.
- **Photography:** real photos may appear in "screen" frames (rounded rectangles with a gloss highlight), like a
  2001 media player.
- **Icons:** glossy 32 px "gel" icons for the seven verbs, with a top highlight band and soft inner shadow.
- **Sparkle:** a 4-point star glyph, with at most 3 per viewport.

### Grid
A 12-column grid with a 32 px gutter, but objects float freely (absolute "stickers") within safe zones. All text
remains in grid columns. Only decorative shapes break it.

## 4. Motion and interaction language

- **Bouncy is off-brand.** The design system bans overshoot, so Y2K here uses *liquid* motion instead: blob morphs
  over 8–12 s with `cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1)`, chrome highlights sliding across type on hover (600 ms).
- **Scroll:** iridescent gradients shift hue angle with scroll (115° → 160°). Chrome numerals rotate ±6° on Y with scroll.
- **Hover:** gel buttons brighten their top highlight and lift 2 px with a soft coloured glow (240 ms). The brand's
  underline-arrow is replaced, in campaign scope only, by a gel capsule.
- **Cursor states:** default = small chrome sphere (12 px, CSS radial gradient) · link = sphere grows to 36 px with an
  iridescent rim · drag (360) = sphere plus orbit ring · view = sphere with sparkle · disabled = frosted grey sphere.
- **Transitions:** "liquid wipe" (SVG goo filter mask, 700 ms). In reduced motion, a fade.
- **Optional:** a soft "startup chime" on first CTA click, muted by default.

## 5. The hero bottle and the four variants

The real glass bottle is the ideal Y2K object: see-through and coloured. It is *not* chromed or re-coloured.

- It floats inside a **frosted capsule** (rounded "pod" panel), as if displayed in a 2000s product kiosk, with float
  ±10 px over 6 s and pointer tilt ±8°. A specular highlight follows the pointer across the glass (an additive
  gradient overlay masked to the bottle alpha).
- Before 360 frames arrive the turn is limited to ±25° with a sheen sweep. Afterwards the 360 viewer gets a
  "turntable" UI: a gel slider beneath the bottle that scrubs frames, a nod to 2000s media-player controls.

| Variant | Y2K "flavour" world |
|---|---|
| **MASTER 26** | "Forest Gel": deep-green translucent blobs, a chrome "26" behind the bottle, frosted green capsule. |
| **ROOT 14** | "Ruby Gel": red translucent blobs, a chrome "14" with a ruby tint on the highlight edge. |
| **BASE 3** | "Amber Gel": amber blobs, a golden-chrome "3", and a warm sunburst lens flare (one, subtle). |
| **ESSENTIAL** | "Pearl": ivory pearlescent, almost no blobs, a satin (not mirror) chrome "E". It is the classiest of the four. |

## 9. Assets needed from DESIGO®

- 360 sequences. The turntable is the centrepiece.
- **Clean photography of the empty returnable bottle** and the return process (crates, washing line if public).
- Real return-loop facts (deposit, pickup cadence), approved. The campaign cannot run on guesses.
- Ghee label scan, wordmark vector and photography as standard.

## 10. Performance, accessibility and mobile

- `backdrop-filter` and SVG filters are expensive. Limit them to 2 frosted layers per viewport, swap to solid frost
  `#E6EEF0` on low-end devices (`navigator.hardwareConcurrency ≤ 4` or `deviceMemory ≤ 4`), and pause blob morphs
  off-screen.
- Chrome text is real text with an SVG paint fallback. In `forced-colors` it becomes CanvasText.
- No flashing iridescence. Hue shifts stay slow (≥ 3 s per cycle) and are WCAG 2.3.1 safe.
- Mobile: pods full-width, 1–2 blobs, gel pills become a vertical list.

## 11. Risks and premium guardrails

**Risks:** soft-drink or cosmetics look; trend fatigue within 12 months; synthetic gloss contradicting "natural";
heavy GPU effects on budget phones.

**Premium guardrails**
1. Never chrome, tint or gel the bottle, the milk or the cows. Y2K effects live around the real objects.
2. Iridescence comes from the four variant light tones only. No rainbow, no neon pink or cyan.
3. Chrome only on numerals and the campaign headline, never on body or prices.
4. One lens flare per page at most, preferably none.
5. No overshoot or bounce (brand motion rule). Y2K energy comes from liquid morphs, not from springiness.
6. Heritage (chapters 10 and 05) stays sincere: no Y2K treatment on breeds' dignity or on history.
7. No playful health or "energy drink" language. Milk is not a power-up.
8. Time-box it: campaign pages carry an end date and the main site never depends on them.
